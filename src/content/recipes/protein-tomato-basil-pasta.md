ingredients:
  - 12 ounces high-protein spaghetti or linguine
  - 1 tablespoon olive oil
  - 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  - 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  - 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  - 1 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more for the pasta water
  - 1 cup cottage cheese
  - 1 packed cup fresh basil leaves, torn
  - 1/2 cup grated Parmesan, plus more for serving

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to its package directions until just shy of al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, then drain.
2. While the pasta cooks, warm the olive oil in a wide skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant and barely golden, about 1 minute.
3. Add the crushed tomatoes, red pepper flakes, and salt. Simmer uncovered for 12 to 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Blend the cottage cheese with 1 cup of the warm tomato sauce until completely smooth. Reduce the skillet to low, stir the blended mixture and Parmesan into the remaining sauce, and warm gently without boiling.
5. Add the drained pasta and half of the basil. Toss over low heat, adding a splash of pasta water until the sauce clings to the noodles. Taste for salt, then finish with the remaining basil and extra Parmesan.

> Chickpea, lentil, and protein-enriched wheat pastas all work. Check the package timing because they cook at different rates.
