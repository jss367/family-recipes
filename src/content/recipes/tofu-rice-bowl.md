ingredients:
  - 1/2 cup jasmine rice
  - 1 cup water, divided
  - 2 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  - 1 small piece fresh ginger, finely chopped
  - 70g broccolini, cut into bite-sized pieces
  - 200g extra-firm tofu, cubed
  - 1 teaspoon potato starch
  - 1 tablespoon plant-based oyster sauce
  - 1/2 tablespoon soy sauce
  - 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  - 1 tablespoon maple syrup
  - Avocado oil, for cooking
  - 1/2 stalk green onion, chopped
  - 1 teaspoon white sesame seeds

## Method

1. Rinse and drain the rice two to three times to remove excess starch. Add 1/2 cup of the water and bring to a gentle boil over medium-high heat, then reduce to medium-low, stir once, cover, and cook for 15 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, finely chop the garlic and ginger, and cut the broccolini into bite-sized pieces. Pat the tofu dry and cut it into cubes.
3. After 15 minutes, turn off the heat under the rice and let it steam, covered, for another 10 minutes.
4. Whisk the potato starch with 1/4 cup of the water to make a slurry, then stir in the oyster sauce, soy sauce, sesame oil, and maple syrup.
5. Heat a nonstick pan over medium heat with a drizzle of avocado oil. Add the tofu and sauté for 3 to 4 minutes, then set aside.
6. Add the broccolini, garlic, and ginger to the same pan and sauté for 2 to 3 minutes.
7. Return the tofu to the pan, pour in the sauce, and stir for about 10 seconds. Add the remaining 1/4 cup water and cook for 3 to 4 minutes more.
8. Serve the tofu and vegetables over the rice, garnished with green onion and sesame seeds.

> A good lazy-night dinner — everything cooks in one pan while the rice steams on its own.
