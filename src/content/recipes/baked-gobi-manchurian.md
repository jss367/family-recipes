ingredients:
  - 1 small head cauliflower, cut into florets (about 4 cups)
  - 7 tablespoons flour (rice or all-purpose)
  - 1/4 cup cornstarch, for the batter
  - 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  - 1/4 teaspoon salt
  - 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  - 2 teaspoons oil, for the batter
  - 1 teaspoon soy sauce, for the batter
  - 1/3 to 1/2 cup water, for the batter
  - 1/4 cup panko
  - 2 teaspoons oil, for the sauce
  - 6 to 8 cloves garlic, finely chopped
  - 1-inch piece ginger, finely chopped
  - 1/2 green or red chili, seeded and finely chopped
  - 1/2 cup finely chopped onion
  - 1/2 cup finely chopped green bell pepper
  - 2 tablespoons finely chopped celery (optional)
  - 1 tablespoon chili sauce, such as sambal oelek
  - 1/4 cup soy sauce, for the sauce
  - 1 tablespoon ketchup
  - 2 teaspoons rice vinegar or apple cider vinegar
  - 1/8 teaspoon black pepper
  - 2 to 3 teaspoons sugar or maple syrup
  - 1 1/2 teaspoons cornstarch, for the slurry
  - 1/2 cup water, for the slurry
  - Sliced green onions, for garnish

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 415°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Whisk together the **flour**, **cornstarch**, **garlic powder**, **salt**, and **baking powder**. Stir in the **oil** and **soy sauce**, then add the **water** a little at a time until the batter is thick and smooth.
3. Toss the **cauliflower** florets in the batter to coat, then sprinkle with the **panko** and toss lightly again. Spread the florets in a single layer on the baking sheet.
4. Bake for 25 to 28 minutes, checking at 25 minutes, until crisp and golden. Remove and set aside.
5. Meanwhile, heat the **oil** for the sauce in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the **garlic**, **ginger**, and **chili**, and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, until the garlic turns golden.
6. Add the **onion**, **bell pepper**, and **celery**, and cook for about 8 minutes, until golden brown.
7. Stir in the **chili sauce**, **soy sauce**, **ketchup**, **vinegar**, **black pepper**, and **sugar**, and bring to a boil.
8. Whisk the **cornstarch** and **water** together to make a slurry, then stir it into the skillet. Cook until the sauce thickens, then taste and adjust soy sauce for salt or chili for heat.
9. Let the sauce cool for about 5 minutes, then add the baked cauliflower and toss to coat.
10. Top with **green onions** and serve right away.

> Cut the florets to a similar size so they bake evenly. If serving later, keep the cauliflower and sauce separate — reheat the cauliflower in the oven for 7 to 9 minutes, then toss it in the warmed sauce just before serving. Swap in firm tofu, rehydrated soy curls, or seitan for some of the cauliflower for extra protein.
