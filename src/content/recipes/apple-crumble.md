ingredients:
  - 6 medium apples, peeled, cored, and sliced
  - 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  - 1/3 cup granulated sugar
  - 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  - 1 tablespoon all-purpose flour
  - 1 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
  - 3/4 cup all-purpose flour
  - 2/3 cup packed brown sugar
  - 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  - 8 tablespoons cold unsalted butter, cubed
---

## Method

1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Butter a 2-quart baking dish.
2. Toss the apples with lemon juice, granulated sugar, cinnamon, and 1 tablespoon flour. Spread evenly in the baking dish.
3. In a bowl, combine the oats, 3/4 cup flour, brown sugar, and salt. Work in the cold butter with your fingertips until the mixture forms uneven crumbs.
4. Scatter the topping over the apples without pressing it down.
5. Bake for 40 to 45 minutes, until the top is golden and the fruit is bubbling at the edges. Rest for 15 minutes before serving.

> A mix of tart and sweet apples gives the filling the best flavor and texture.
