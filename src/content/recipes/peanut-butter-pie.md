  - 8 oz cream cheese, one package
  - 1 1/2 cups confectioners' sugar
  - 1 cup peanut butter
  - 1 cup milk
  - 16 oz frozen whipped topping, thawed, one package
  - 2 prepared 9-inch graham cracker crusts
---

## Method

1. Beat the cream cheese and confectioners' sugar together in a large bowl with an electric mixer until smooth and creamy.
2. Add the peanut butter and milk and beat until smooth.
3. Fold in the whipped topping until well combined, then spoon the mixture evenly into the graham cracker crusts.
4. Cover and freeze until firm.
