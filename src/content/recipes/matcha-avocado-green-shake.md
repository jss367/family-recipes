ingredients:
  - 2 packed cups baby spinach
  - 1/2 ripe avocado
  - 1 frozen banana
  - 1 teaspoon matcha powder
  - 1 tablespoon hemp seeds
  - 1 1/2 cups unsweetened oat milk
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  - Pinch of kosher salt
  - 1/2 cup ice
---

## Method

1. Add the oat milk and spinach to a blender and blend until completely smooth.
2. Add the avocado, banana, matcha, hemp seeds, vanilla, salt, and ice.
3. Blend until thick and creamy. Divide between two glasses and serve right away.

> Start with 1/2 teaspoon matcha if you prefer a gentler tea flavor or less caffeine.
