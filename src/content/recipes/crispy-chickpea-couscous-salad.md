ingredients:
  - 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  - 2 tablespoons Italian seasoning, divided
  - Olive oil, for roasting and the dressing
  - Salt and pepper, to taste
  - 5 oz Israeli couscous
  - 1 1/2 cups water
  - 2 tablespoons butter
  - 1/2 cup panko breadcrumbs
  - 8 oz kale, stems removed and chopped
  - 8 oz grape tomatoes, halved
  - 10 teaspoons balsamic glaze
  - 4 teaspoons Dijon mustard
  - 6 tablespoons shredded parmesan cheese
---

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 425°F with a rack in the top position. Pat the drained, rinsed chickpeas very dry, then toss them on a baking sheet with a drizzle of olive oil, half the Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Roast on the top rack until golden and tender, 18 to 20 minutes.
2. Meanwhile, combine the couscous, water, some of the remaining Italian seasoning, and a pinch of salt in a small pot. Bring to a boil, then cover, reduce the heat to low, and cook until tender, 6 to 8 minutes. Drain if needed and spread on a plate to cool.
3. While the couscous cooks, melt the butter in a medium pan over medium-high heat. Add the panko, the rest of the Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper, and toast, stirring, until light golden, 2 to 3 minutes. Transfer to a plate — it darkens a bit more as it cools.
4. Remove any large stems from the kale and chop it into bite-sized pieces. Halve the tomatoes. Put the kale in a large bowl, season with a pinch of salt, and massage with your hands until the leaves are tender, about 1 minute — this softens the kale without leaving it fibrous.
5. Whisk together the balsamic glaze, mustard, and a few tablespoons of olive oil in a small bowl until smooth. Season with salt and pepper.
6. Add the tomatoes, cooled couscous, roasted chickpeas, and dressing to the bowl with the kale. Toss until evenly coated, taste, and adjust the seasoning. Divide between bowls and top with the toasted panko and parmesan.
