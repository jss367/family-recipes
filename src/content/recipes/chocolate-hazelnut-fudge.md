ingredients:
  - 2/3 cup hazelnuts
  - 8 oz sweetened condensed milk, one can
  - 12 oz dark chocolate chips or chunks (60% to 70% cacao)
  - 1/2 cup creamy almond butter
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  - Flaky sea salt

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Spread the hazelnuts on a rimmed baking sheet and roast for 10 to 13 minutes, until fragrant.
2. Wrap the hot hazelnuts tightly in a clean dish towel and let steam for 2 minutes, then rub vigorously to loosen the skins. Let cool, then roughly chop.
3. Line an 8x4-inch loaf pan for thicker pieces, or an 8x8-inch pan for thinner ones, with parchment paper.
4. Combine the condensed milk and chocolate in a microwave-safe bowl. Microwave on high for 90 seconds, let sit undisturbed for 1 minute, then whisk until smooth.
5. While still warm, whisk in the almond butter, vanilla, and a pinch of sea salt until thick and fully combined.
6. Fold in the chopped hazelnuts.
7. Spread the mixture evenly in the prepared pan and sprinkle lightly with sea salt.
8. Refrigerate for 1 to 2 hours, until set. Let come to room temperature before slicing.

> **To make it vegan,** swap in an 8 oz can of sweetened condensed coconut milk and use dairy-free chocolate — many dark chocolate chips contain milk fat. Condensed coconut milk sets up closest to the classic fudge texture; condensed oat milk works too, but comes out a bit stiffer.
>
> Work quickly while the chocolate mixture is still warm, since it firms up fast. A darker chocolate, closer to 70%, makes for a less sweet fudge. Wrapped tightly, it keeps at room temperature up to 2 weeks, longer in the fridge, or up to 3 months in the freezer.
