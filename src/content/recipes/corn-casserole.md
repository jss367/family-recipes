## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 350°F.
2. Stir together the **creamed corn**, drained **whole kernel corn**, **corn muffin mix**, **sour cream**, and melted **butter** in a large bowl.
3. Pour the mixture into a parchment-lined 9x13-inch baking dish.
4. Bake uncovered for 55 to 60 minutes, until golden.
5. Let rest for 5 minutes before serving.

> Add up to 2 tablespoons of sugar if you like it sweeter. This can be assembled ahead and refrigerated up to 2 days — let it sit at room temperature for about 30 minutes before baking. Leftovers keep 3 to 4 days in the fridge, or freeze up to 3 months, though it's best fresh.
