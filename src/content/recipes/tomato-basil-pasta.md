ingredients:
  - 12 ounces spaghetti or linguine
  - 2 tablespoons olive oil
  - 3 garlic cloves, thinly sliced
  - 1 can (28 ounces) crushed tomatoes
  - 1/2 teaspoon red pepper flakes
  - 1 teaspoon kosher salt, plus more for the pasta water
  - 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  - 1 packed cup fresh basil leaves, torn
  - Grated Parmesan, for serving
---

## Method

1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Cook the pasta until just shy of al dente. Reserve 1 cup of pasta water, then drain.
2. While the pasta cooks, warm the olive oil in a wide skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook until fragrant and barely golden, about 1 minute.
3. Add the crushed tomatoes, red pepper flakes, and salt. Simmer uncovered for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally.
4. Add the drained pasta, butter, and half of the basil. Toss over low heat, adding a splash of pasta water until the sauce clings to the noodles.
5. Taste for salt. Finish with the remaining basil and serve with Parmesan.

> The sauce can be made two days ahead. Reheat it gently before adding freshly cooked pasta.
