  - 1 tablespoon olive oil
  - 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
  - 1/2 teaspoon onion powder
  - 1/2 teaspoon paprika (regular or smoked)
  - 1/4 teaspoon salt
  - 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  - 1 tablespoon butter
  - 1/2 lemon

## Method

1. Pat the **salmon** dry. Lightly coat the flesh with a little of the **olive oil**, then sprinkle evenly with the **garlic powder**, **onion powder**, **paprika**, **salt**, and **black pepper**.
2. Heat the remaining olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the salmon skin-side down and cook for 5 to 7 minutes.
3. Flip and cook for another 2 to 4 minutes, depending on thickness, until the thickest part reaches **145°F**. Keep the heat moderate so the spices do not scorch; lower it if they brown too quickly. Add the **butter** during the last minute and spoon it over the fish.
4. Take the pan off the heat, squeeze the **lemon** over the salmon, and serve.

> Serve with rice and steamed broccoli if you like. Microwave rice and frozen broccoli keep the sides simple; prepare them according to their package instructions while the salmon cooks. Sides are not included in the ingredient checklist.
