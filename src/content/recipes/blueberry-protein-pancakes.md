ingredients:
  - 1 1/2 cups oat flour
  - 1/2 cup vanilla or unflavored protein powder
  - 2 teaspoons baking powder
  - 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  - 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  - 1 cup cottage cheese
  - 3 large eggs
  - 3/4 cup milk, plus more as needed
  - 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  - 1 cup fresh or frozen blueberries
  - Butter or neutral oil, for the pan
  - Maple syrup, for serving
---

## Method

1. Whisk the oat flour, protein powder, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt in a large bowl.
2. Blend the cottage cheese, eggs, milk, and vanilla until smooth. Pour into the dry ingredients and fold just until combined, then gently fold in the blueberries.
3. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes while a griddle heats over medium-low. If the batter becomes too thick to scoop, stir in milk 1 tablespoon at a time.
4. Lightly butter the griddle. Scoop 1/3 cup batter per pancake and cook until bubbles form and the edges look set, 2 to 3 minutes. Flip and cook until golden and cooked through, 1 to 2 minutes more.
5. Serve immediately with maple syrup.

> Keep the heat at medium-low. Protein-rich batter browns more quickly than traditional pancake batter.
