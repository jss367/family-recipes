ingredients:
  - 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for drizzling
  - 1 large yellow onion, diced
  - 1 medium red bell pepper, chopped
  - 6 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  - 1 cinnamon stick (optional)
  - 1 tablespoon ras el hanout
  - 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  - 1/2 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  - 1/2 teaspoon ground cumin
  - 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
  - 1/4 teaspoon ground turmeric
  - 1/4 to 1/2 teaspoon cayenne pepper (optional)
  - Pinch of ground cloves
  - Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
  - 2 cups vegetable broth
  - 3 cups butternut squash, peeled and finely diced
  - 2 (15 oz) cans chickpeas, drained and rinsed
  - 2 bay leaves
  - 14.5 oz crushed tomatoes, one can
  - 3 to 6 Medjool dates, chopped
  - 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  - 1 handful flat-leaf parsley or cilantro, chopped
  - 5 oz plain vegan yogurt, 2 teaspoons lemon juice, and 1/2 teaspoon ground coriander, for the yogurt sauce (optional)

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Stir together the ras el hanout, coriander, cinnamon, cumin, ginger, turmeric, cayenne, and cloves in a small bowl.
2. Heat the olive oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat. Add the onion with a pinch of salt and cook until golden, 7 to 8 minutes, adding a splash of water if it starts to catch.
3. Add the bell pepper, garlic, and cinnamon stick and cook for 3 minutes, stirring frequently.
4. Stir in the spice mix and cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute, adding a little water if the pan looks dry.
5. Pour in a splash of the broth to deglaze, scraping up any browned bits. Add the remaining broth, squash, chickpeas, bay leaves, salt, tomatoes, and dates and bring to a boil, then remove from the heat.
6. Drizzle generously with olive oil, stir, and cover tightly with a lid or foil.
7. Transfer to the oven and bake covered for about 40 minutes, until the squash is tender and the mixture has thickened into a stew.
8. Meanwhile, stir together the yogurt, lemon juice, coriander, and a pinch of salt for the yogurt sauce.
9. Let the tagine cool for 15 to 20 minutes, then stir in half the lemon zest and the lemon juice. Taste and adjust the seasoning.
10. Serve topped with the yogurt sauce, fresh herbs, and a drizzle of olive oil.

> Dice the squash finely so it has time to soften during the bake, and use a real cinnamon stick if you have one — it makes a noticeable difference over ground cinnamon alone. Swap golden raisins or dried apricots for the dates, sweet potato for the squash, or white beans for the chickpeas. Leftovers keep in the fridge up to 5 days and freeze well; thin with a little water or broth when reheating.
