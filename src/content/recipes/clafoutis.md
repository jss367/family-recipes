ingredients:
  - 12 oz fresh raspberries, blueberries, blackberries, or sliced strawberries, or 1 lb dark cherries, pitted and halved
  - 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  - 1 cup almond milk or whole milk
  - 1/4 cup heavy cream
  - 1/3 cup granulated sugar, plus 1 tablespoon for topping
  - 3 eggs
  - 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  - 1/2 teaspoon almond extract
  - 1/4 teaspoon fine salt
  - 1/2 cup all-purpose flour
  - Whipped cream or vanilla ice cream, for serving (optional)
---

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F.
2. Melt the butter in a 10-inch cast iron skillet or 9.5-inch pie plate over medium heat, tilting to coat the sides. Arrange the fruit in a single layer in the pan and set aside.
3. Blend the milk, cream, 1/3 cup sugar, eggs, vanilla, almond extract, salt, and flour on medium speed for about 30 seconds, until smooth and frothy.
4. Pour the batter over the fruit and sprinkle the remaining tablespoon of sugar on top.
5. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, until puffed and golden. Let cool before serving warm, at room temperature, or chilled.

> Fresh fruit works best here — frozen fruit releases extra moisture and can make the clafoutis fall apart after the first day. No blender? A hand mixer on high speed works too. For a dairy-free version, swap in a neutral oil or vegan butter and use a little more almond milk in place of the cream. Leftovers keep in the fridge up to 4 days.
