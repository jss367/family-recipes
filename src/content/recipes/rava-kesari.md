ingredients:
  - 1/2 cup rava (semolina)
  - 1/4 cup ghee
  - 2 tablespoons chopped cashews
  - 1 cup sugar
  - 1 1/2 cups water
  - 1/2 teaspoon kesari or orange food coloring
  - 7 to 8 saffron strands
  - 1/2 teaspoon ground cardamom
---

## Method

1. In a small pressure cooker, combine the ghee, rava, and cashews over low to medium heat. At the same time, in a separate pan, combine the water, sugar, food coloring, saffron, and cardamom, and bring to a simmer.
2. Stir the rava mixture continuously for 3 to 5 minutes, until the cashews turn golden, which signals the rava is toasted.
3. Once the sugar has fully dissolved in the syrup, turn off the heat under both.
4. Carefully pour the hot syrup into the roasted rava in the pressure cooker.
5. Close the cooker with the weight in place and let it sit off the heat for 20 minutes — the retained heat finishes the cooking.

> The rava, sugar, and water scale in a 1:2:3 ratio, so this is easy to size up or down. Ghee can go up to 1/3 cup but shouldn't drop below a tablespoon. A neutral oil works in place of ghee for a vegan version, though the flavor changes. Stir some chopped fruit into the syrup for a fruit kesari variation.
