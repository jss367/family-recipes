## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
2. Mix the crumbled **tempeh** with the **olive oil**, a pinch of **salt**, and the **sambal oelek**, then spread it over one half of the baking sheet.
3. Roast for 6 minutes, then toss the **broccoli** florets with the remaining **olive oil**, **salt**, and **pepper**, and spread them over the other half of the sheet.
4. Return to the oven and roast both for another 12 minutes (18 minutes total for the tempeh, 12 minutes for the broccoli), stirring each half around the midpoint of its own cook time.
5. Meanwhile, cook the **rice** according to the package directions.
6. Toast the **shredded coconut** in a dry skillet over medium heat, stirring frequently, until golden brown, then stir it into the cooked rice with a pinch of salt.
7. Whisk together the **peanut butter**, **lime juice**, **sambal oelek**, **garlic**, **maple syrup**, **water**, and a pinch of **salt** until smooth and pourable, thinning with a little more water if needed.
8. If using, blanch the **snap peas** in boiling water for 1 to 2 minutes, until bright green and crisp-tender, then drain.
9. Plate the coconut rice, top with the roasted tempeh and snap peas, and serve the broccoli alongside. Drizzle generously with the peanut sauce.
