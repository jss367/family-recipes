## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 400°F.
2. Heat **1 tablespoon olive oil** over medium-high heat in a thick-bottomed sauté pan. Add the sliced **onions**, stir to coat, and cook until golden brown, about 15 to 20 minutes.
3. Put the sliced **potatoes** in a large bowl and gently toss with the remaining **1 tablespoon olive oil**, **1/2 cup Gruyère**, the **Parmesan**, **salt**, and **white pepper**.
4. Lightly butter a large gratin dish (2 to 3 quart capacity) or a 9 x 13-inch casserole. Line the bottom with the **caramelized onions** in an even layer.
5. Arrange the cheese-coated potato slices in a pattern over the onions. Pour the **vegetable stock** over the potatoes and sprinkle with the remaining **1/4 cup Gruyère**.
6. Cover with aluminum foil and bake for 50 minutes, or until fork tender.
7. Remove the foil and broil for 5 minutes, uncovered, until lightly browned.
8. Sprinkle with **rosemary** (or **thyme** or **chives**) to serve.

> Serves 6 to 8. If you're making this ahead, make sure the potatoes are well coated in olive oil, adding more if needed, to keep them from turning grey. Prep and cook times are estimates. Gruyère and Parmesan are traditionally made with animal rennet, so look for vegetarian-labeled versions. Cheese swaps: for the Gruyère, use Comté, Emmental or Swiss, Fontina, or a sharp white cheddar or aged Gouda; for the Parmesan, use Grana Padano or a little less Pecorino Romano, which is saltier.
