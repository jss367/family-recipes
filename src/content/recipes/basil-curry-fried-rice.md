  - 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
  - 1 small piece fresh ginger, finely chopped
  - 3 tablespoons chili oil
  - 1 1/2 tablespoons soy sauce, divided
  - 1 teaspoon dark soy sauce
  - 1 1/2 cups cooked basmati rice, preferably day-old
  - 1 tablespoon curry powder
  - 10g Thai basil leaves
---

## Method

1. Pat the tofu dry and crumble it with a fork in a bowl.
2. Finely chop the garlic and ginger.
3. Heat the chili oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat.
4. Add the garlic, ginger, and crumbled tofu and sauté for 5 to 8 minutes.
5. Stir in 1 tablespoon of the soy sauce and the dark soy sauce, and sauté for 2 minutes more.
6. Add the rice, curry powder, and the remaining soy sauce, and sauté for another 1 to 2 minutes.
7. Add the basil, turn off the heat, and fold it into the rice until wilted.

> Day-old rice works best here since it's drier and fries up rather than turning mushy.
