ingredients:
  - 1 lb peaches, ripe but firm
  - 1/2 cup fresh blueberries
  - 1/3 cup brown sugar or coconut sugar
  - 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  - 1/4 teaspoon fresh grated nutmeg
  - Scant 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger
  - 1/8 teaspoon ground cardamom (optional)
  - 10 tablespoons vegan butter
  - 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  - Scant 2/3 cup old-fashioned rolled oats
  - 1 cup cane sugar
  - Scant 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  - 2 teaspoons baking powder
  - 1 1/4 cups full-fat oat milk
  - 1 1/2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  - 1/4 teaspoon almond extract (optional)
  - Vegan vanilla ice cream, for serving (optional)

## Method

1. Preheat the oven to 375°F with a rack in the middle.
2. Halve, pit, and slice the peaches into 1/4- to 1/2-inch pieces. Toss with the blueberries, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg, ginger, and cardamom, and let sit for about 30 minutes so the fruit releases its juices.
3. Melt the vegan butter in a 12-inch cast iron skillet over medium heat, swirling it up the sides. Once foamy, keep cooking for another couple of minutes, stirring often, until browned and fragrant, then remove from the heat.
4. Whisk together the flour, oats, cane sugar, salt, and baking powder in a separate bowl. Add the oat milk and vanilla and almond extracts, if using, and fold just until combined.
5. Dollop the batter over the browned butter in several spots so it swirls together. Top with the peaches and blueberries, then spoon over any juices left in the bowl.
6. Bake for 45 to 50 minutes, rotating the pan halfway through, until deeply golden and bubbling at the edges.
7. Let cool on a wire rack for 10 to 20 minutes, then serve warm, with ice cream if you like.

> Keep an eye on the butter as it browns so it doesn't scorch — it's ready once it smells nutty. Most vegan butters won't brown quite like dairy butter, but they still turn richer and more flavorful. No cast iron? Brown the butter in a separate skillet, then bake in a 2- to 3-quart dish instead. Blackberries or raspberries can stand in for the blueberries, and nectarines for the peaches.
