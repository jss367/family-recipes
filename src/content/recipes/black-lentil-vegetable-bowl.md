## Method

1. Put the **lentils** in a small saucepan and cover generously with water. Bring to a boil, reduce to a simmer, and cook until tender but intact, about 20 to 25 minutes. Drain well.
2. While the lentils cook, heat the **olive oil** in a large skillet with a lid over medium-high heat. Add the **mushrooms** in a single layer and leave undisturbed for 2 to 3 minutes. Stir and continue cooking until browned and any released water has evaporated.
3. Add the **garlic**, **ginger**, and **cumin** and stir for about 30 seconds.
4. Add the **broccoli**, **cauliflower**, and **water**. Cover, reduce the heat to medium, and cook until tender, about 5 to 8 minutes. Check occasionally and add a splash of water if the pan dries out before the vegetables are tender.
5. Uncover and let any remaining water evaporate. Stir in the drained **lentils** and cook until warmed through.
6. Transfer to a bowl, squeeze over the **lime**, and add the **apple cider vinegar**. Toss gently and finish with the **hemp seeds** and **kimchi**.

> For a softer texture, keep the skillet covered and cook the broccoli and cauliflower for 2 to 3 minutes longer, adding a splash of water as needed.
