ingredients:
  - 2 cups all-purpose flour
  - 2 tablespoons granulated sugar
  - 2 teaspoons baking powder
  - 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  - 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  - 2 cups buttermilk
  - 2 large eggs
  - 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted and cooled
  - Butter or neutral oil, for the pan
  - Maple syrup and fruit, for serving
---

## Method

1. Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt in a large bowl.
2. In a second bowl, whisk the buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients and fold just until no dry streaks remain. A few lumps are fine.
3. Let the batter rest for 5 minutes while a griddle heats over medium-low.
4. Lightly butter the griddle. Scoop 1/3 cup batter per pancake and cook until bubbles form and the edges look set, 2 to 3 minutes. Flip and cook until golden, 1 to 2 minutes more.
5. Serve immediately with maple syrup and fruit.

> Keep finished pancakes warm on a sheet pan in a 200°F oven while you cook the remaining batches.
