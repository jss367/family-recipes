ingredients:
  - 1 cup Greek yogurt
  - 1 garlic clove, finely grated
  - Kosher salt and black pepper, to taste
  - 4 large eggs
  - 1 tablespoon white vinegar or apple cider vinegar
  - 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  - 2 teaspoons Aleppo pepper
  - 1 teaspoon paprika
  - Chopped fresh herbs, for garnish (dill, parsley, mint, etc.)

## Method

1. Stir together the yogurt, garlic, salt, and pepper in a small bowl and set aside.
2. Melt the butter in a small pan over medium-low heat, then stir in the Aleppo pepper and paprika for about a minute before turning off the heat.
3. Crack each egg into a small strainer set over a bowl to drain off the watery whites, then transfer each drained egg to its own small ramekin.
4. Bring a medium pot of water to a bare simmer over medium heat and stir in the vinegar. Reduce the heat to medium-low so only a few bubbles rise from the bottom. Gently slide each egg from its ramekin into the water, spacing them a few inches apart, and cook for 3 minutes. Lift out with a slotted spoon and set on a paper towel-lined plate.
5. Spread the garlicky yogurt on a plate, top with the poached eggs, drizzle with the spiced butter, and garnish with fresh herbs.

> No Aleppo pepper on hand? Sweet paprika with a pinch of cayenne, or paprika with red pepper flakes, both work as substitutes. The yogurt and poached eggs can be made up to two days ahead — keep the cooked eggs in cold water in the fridge and rewarm them in hot water for about 20 seconds before serving. Serve with crusty bread, Turkish flatbread, or pita for scooping up the sauce.
